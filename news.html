January 2026

Journal of Fluid Mechanics Paper Published

Breaking symmetry isn’t always a bad thing. Sometimes, it’s exactly what you need to gain control or to deliberately let go of periodic behavior. Check out our recent Journal of Fluid Mechanics article, where we show how breaking the inherent axisymmetry of a globally unstable jet can drastically suppress instabilities, while preserving axisymmetry can instead amplify oscillation amplitudes.
