Research Interests

  • → Experimental investigation of next generation superhydrophobic surface and to unravel how surface features, chemical hetrogenity, and charge accumulation due to slide electrification affect the friction forces and hydrophobicity
  • → Experimetnal study of Hydrodynamic instability and thermoacoustic instability, and open-loop control of their nonlinear dynamics
  • → Advanced experimental diagnostics and measurement techniques, including Hotwire Anemometry, Time-resolved Stereoscopic PIV, Reflection Interference Contrast Microscopy (RICM)
  • → Design and development of energy efficient fuel-flexible combustion systems

I am particularly interested in establishing an experimental research laboratory focused on combustion and fluid dynamics with strong potential for interdisciplinary collaboration.
