About the Research

Designing Actively Switchable Superhydrophobic Surfaces

This project investigates the design and fabrication of actively switchable superhydrophobic surfaces and explores how droplets move, slide, and rotate on engineered non-wetting surfaces. The fundamental insights will be translated towards applications in drag reduction and fluid–surface interaction control.
