About

I am an experimental fluid dynamicist specializing in advanced optical diagnostics, droplet dynamics, and flow control. My research integrates precision experiments-such as stereoscopic PIV, high-speed imaging, interferometry, and microfabrication-with theoretical modeling to uncover the fundamental physics of complex flows and liquid-surface interactions. I am a recipient of the prestigious Ramanujan Fellowship awarded by the ANRF, Government of India.

I earned my Ph.D. from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2021). My PhD dissertation was investigation of the temporal and spatiotemporal dynamics of globally unstable jets subjected to different forcing symmetries- axial, transverse, and their combinations. I completed my Master’s degree in Aerospace Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur (2016). During my PhD studies, I spent a year as a visiting scholar at the Thermofluids Research Group, NTNU, Norway.

My work spans a broad spectrum-from the control of combustion-driven instabilities and open-loop manipulation of globally unstable flows to micro-Newton-scale friction measurements and droplet dynamics on non-wetting (superhydrophobic and superheated) surfaces. I view these diverse topics as interconnected components of a larger physical framework that links fluid mechanics across scales and applications. My research aims to advance both fundamental understanding and practical design strategies in microfluidics, aerospace, biomedical systems, and industrial fluid transport.

I enjoy traveling, hiking, and taking spontaneous road trips with my family. Pizza is my go-to comfort food, though I often turns to cooking home-style meals during extended research stays abroad.

Research Focus Areas

01

Hydrodynamic Instabilities

Experimental study of nonlinear dynamics and vortex dynamics of globally unstable jets and their control strategies

02

Droplet Dynamics, Electrospray, and Encapsulations

Unraveling the dynamics of micro-droplets on non-wetting surfaces for advanced applications

03

Nature Inspired Engineering

Experimental study of bio-inspired designs for improving the aerodynamic performance of aerofoils and drag-reducing through the surface modification by microfabrication techniques, including photolithograpgy, additive manufacturing, and Femtosecond laser processing.
