Experimental Thermo-Fluid Dynamics

About

I am a mechanical engineering researcher specializing in experimental investigations of combustion systems and fluid dynamics phenomena. My work focuses on advancing our understanding of complex thermofluid systems through rigorous experimental methods and innovative diagnostic techniques.

Currently, I am pursuing faculty positions where I can establish a research program dedicated to sustainable energy systems, combustion science, and advanced fluid mechanics. My research philosophy emphasizes bridging fundamental science with practical applications in energy conversion and environmental sustainability.

My expertise spans experimental combustion, thermoacoustic instabilities, droplet dynamics, and advanced measurement techniques including high-speed imaging and laser diagnostics. I am particularly interested in developing next-generation combustor technologies that enable fuel flexibility while minimizing environmental impact.
